# CASM paper v5 variants

All three variants are derived from `main/casm_v4.tex`. They share the same
experiments, results, conclusion, bibliography, and figures. Their substantive
difference is the Introduction and the opening of the Methodology.

Common structural changes:

- the standalone Related Work section is removed and its essential positioning
  is integrated into the Introduction;
- DBN, PLPDP, the causal 1D semi-Markov model, and BeatNet/BeatNet+ are given
  explicit and non-overlapping roles;
- the former high-level CASM overview is moved into the Introduction;
- the former detailed Introduction explanation now opens the CASM Overview;
- both Figure 1 and Figure 2 are placed in the Methodology;
- the last sentence of the Introduction states the methodological and empirical
  contribution explicitly.

Variant emphasis:

- `v5a`: balanced, conventional reviewer-facing narrative;
- `v5b`: reader-first problem/solution narrative with the clearest verbal
  explanation of the decoding trade-off;
- `v5c`: compact, novelty-boundary-first narrative for tighter page budgets.

Each variant is self-contained: its folder includes the TeX source, compiled
PDF, bibliography, conference style, bibliography style, and figures.
