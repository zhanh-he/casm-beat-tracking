# Archive

This is the fourth top-level area of the project. Its full contents are kept
locally by the authors but intentionally excluded from the public repository:
they are not required to install, use, test, or reproduce the published result
tables, and some items are third-party reading material.

- `manuscript-workspace/`: manuscript drafts, notes, reading material, and build artifacts.
- `presentation/`: the project presentation source.
- `figure-workspaces/`: historical figure generators, candidate galleries, and large intermediate result bundles.
- `maintainer/`: release and packaging checklists for maintainers.

The reader-facing package, experiments, and demo remain in the three sibling
top-level directories. This README is published to document where the private
research history went; archived files are retained locally only for provenance
and recovery.
