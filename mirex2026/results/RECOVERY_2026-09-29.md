## BeatThis: distinct 100-epoch full-allowed-data baseline

The separate seed-0, 100-epoch baseline trained without validation on all
allowed pieces (13,688 training excerpts, still no SMC/GTZAN). It is not the
epoch-79 single-split checkpoint. The corrected, disjoint diagnostic in
[`BASELINE_DIAGNOSTIC.md`](BASELINE_DIAGNOSTIC.md) gives Direct beat F of
58.86% on 217 SMC pieces and 89.58% on 993 GTZAN pieces; no-SMC CASM gives
58.94% and 89.62%. Upstream `final0` Direct is comparable on GTZAN **only**:
89.22% beat F. The paper's 62.70% BeatThis SMC out-of-fold Direct score uses
fold models trained on other SMC pieces, so it is not a matched no-SMC baseline.
This baseline is useful but follows the preliminary seed-0/100-epoch recipe,
not the as-yet-unfrozen expanded-search winner.
